How it works

Small profits, over and over.

A grid bot does not try to predict where the market is going. It earns from the price moving up and down inside a range.

  1. Draw a grid

    The bot splits a price range into evenly spaced levels and puts buy orders on the levels below the current price.

  2. Buy low, sell one step higher

    When a buy order fills, the bot places a sell order one level above it. When that sells, the gap between the two prices, minus fees, is the profit for that cycle.

  3. Follow the price

    The grid is dynamic and trailing. If the price leaves the range, the bot shifts the grid so it keeps working around where the market is now.

  4. Count what really happened

    Profit is calculated from the actual fill prices and fees reported by the exchange, not from estimates, and a live card checks it against the real account balance.

What I built

More than a script that places orders.

Live dashboard

Real-time view

An Express server streams orders, fills and profit to a dashboard over WebSockets, so I can follow the bot without refreshing.

Accuracy

Real fills, real numbers

Each grid level remembers its true entry price and fee, so profit on the sell side uses the actual cost. A balance card shows whether the bot's books match Binance.

Reliability

Built to keep running

State is saved safely and flushed at critical moments, the bot shuts down cleanly, and a watchdog detects WebSocket connections that have silently died.

Security

Locked down on purpose

Signed session tokens, timing-safe checks and login rate limiting protect the controls, and the whole app is only reachable from my own devices.

Why there is no live link

It trades real money, so it stays private.

The bot runs on the real Binance exchange, not on a testnet or a simulation. Because of that, its dashboard is only reachable through my private Tailscale network. That is why this page explains the bot instead of linking to it.
